Ran Wei

Assistant Professor

Graduate School of Education, Peking University

Her research focuses on language and cognitive development in childhood, with an emphasis on parent–child interaction. Her work also examines future thinking and self-regulation, and the role of artificial intelligence in children’s learning and development.
